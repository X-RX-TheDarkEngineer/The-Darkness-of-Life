# The Darkness of Life

A Kingdom Hearts: Chain of Memories-inspired pixel card battler, built from scratch in HTML, CSS and JavaScript. The story is about Xado_RemnantX.

## The story

An urban, dark, overpopulated, Gotham-style, post-apocalyptic, futuristic city. Large city gates lock the city off from the Dissonance — the void abysmal wasteland that comes to life with darkness.

Xado_RemnantX — also known as Xado, Remnant, RemnantX, or RX — is a retired mercenary of the diminished, lost souls like Nobodies or Heartless entities, fighting his way out of the darkness. One of the few to survive complete destruction and shattering of his soul, now pieced together and realigned. He retired after the War of Death, the battle that ended the common world, life before the apocalypse.

He is a lone wolf, a black wolf warrior spirit, and doesn't shift as often anymore. He walks alone after losing his lover, whom he devoted himself to save, until she betrayed him and lost herself in the process. Broken and reserved, but strong as his integrity, intellect and resilience shines through him. He IS the light in the darkness.

> "We walk the dark, rise. We walk with truth and honor. We tend and earn our place. Though he walks through the valley of the shadow of death, he shall fear no evil, fore evil, is not pure against those worthy of a soul of light."

## How to play

Open `index.html` in a browser for the title screen, or `level1.html` for the first level. Move with WASD or arrow keys.

## Project structure

- `index.html` — Step 1: intro title screen
- `level1.html` — Step 2: first level, idle, pixel movement with velocity, friction and tile collision
- `assets/main-character.jpg` — Xado_RemnantX, the main character
- `world-bible.md` — the full world and character lore
